Haochen Yang

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, advised by Prof. Hongkai Yu. Before joining UTK, I pursued my Ph.D. study in Computer Science at Cleveland State University. I received my M.S. in Computer Science and Technology from ShanghaiTech University and my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Jinan.

My research interests include autonomous driving, embodied intelligence, and multimodal learning. My work has been published in leading computer vision, artificial intelligence, and robotics venues, including CVPR, ECCV, AAAI, ACM MM, and ICRA.
